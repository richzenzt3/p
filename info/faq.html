FAQ

  • “How can I help?” You can help by sharing the link to this project, sharing missing videos, mirroring it, etc. You can also share your own collection and it will be posted here (just as mine).
  • “I want to help with money" This will always remain FREE and there's no profit. If you really want to donate and ask what you want, you can go to Other -> Donation.
  • "How is each folder organized?” Each folder constains videos in the format "[creator] [year] video_name" (where "creator" and "year" may be unavailable if i don’t know those, and video_name is the original file name or "unkown" if i dont know it).
  • “What to expect from the videos?” Mostly White and NN (no HMV, JAV, Asian, Black, Hardcore, Anal [maybe a minimum amount if the overall video is good]). There’s no Trans, Gay, Sissy, Femboy, except in the "other" sub-folder (present mostly in the most famous ones). 1080p are preferred over upscales (which are present only if it's the only video i could find).
  • “Why it's missing a lot of videos?" Because only the best ones are being saved (otherwise we'll be in lack of space). The exception to this rule is the sub-folder "other" (present only in some folders).
